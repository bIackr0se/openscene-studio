# OpenScene Studio narration guide

The final film uses one clear external narrator. The woman in the station video remains silent. Read at a calm 135 to 145 words per minute and leave the visual pauses in the edit instead of slowing individual words.

## Script

1. `fixed-video-problem`

   Asking for help in another language is hard, especially when a passenger cannot use stairs, hear an announcement, or read a sign.

2. `open-project`

   This German lesson says the next train leaves from platform two, but never teaches how to ask for the lift.

3. `human-request`

   The learner cannot use stairs. Add the trainer-approved German lift question and recorded answer to this OpenScene lesson, then preview the learner's turn.

4. `page-owned-proposal`

   ChatGPT reads the open OpenScene project and selects the trainer's approved lift answer. OpenScene keeps the words, route board, video, and timing fixed.

5. `runnable-draft`

   The draft adds the learner's need, the German phrase, and the pause before the filmed answer.

6. `human-turn`

   When the trainer previews the path, the video stops before the answer. The learner must choose what to say.

7. `response-release`

   The wrong phrase leaves the answer locked. The matching phrase starts the trainer-approved filmed answer.

8. `project-context`

   Now the learner has practised the phrase inside the station situation, and the route board shows where the lift leads.

9. `human-decision`

   The trainer keeps the new path or restores the earlier lesson.

10. `tool-contract`

    Six WebMCP tools let ChatGPT inspect and edit the same OpenScene project shown here.

11. `broader-use`

    OpenScene can add other trainer-approved practice paths for mobility, hearing, language, or cognitive needs without filming the whole lesson again.

## Delivery

- Start each passage after its visual subject has settled.
- Pause 0.6 to 1.0 seconds between ideas.
- Leave 1.2 to 1.8 seconds of clean room around the correct learner click and response onset.
- Hold the human request for two seconds after it finishes.
- Hold each native tool input and structured result for at least 2.5 seconds.
- Hold the answer board for at least four seconds.
- Keep sentence-internal phrasing natural. Reject any take with clipped consonants, doubled words, stutters, or inserted pauses inside a phrase.
- Use clean silence beneath quiet holds. Do not add room tone, hiss, click effects, or a music bed.
- Burn in English captions, two lines at most, outside the tool-evidence area.

## Voice selection

Use a natural licensed voice with clear consonants, calm authority, and no announcer affect. Compare the same first three passages in the user's own read and the best available licensed ChatGPT voice. Keep the clearer, warmer performance after a blind listen at laptop volume. Do not use a macOS system voice for the final film.

Use the installed free local Superwhisper model only to check intelligibility, the transcript, and caption timing. Do not select a Pro or cloud model or change a Superwhisper account setting.

## Truth boundary

- The narrator speaks about the product. The woman in the station video has no audio.
- Do not imply lip synchronization, speech recognition, character perception, or live travel guidance.
- Do not describe the page receipt as native ChatGPT proof. The film must show the real ChatGPT tool surface.
- Re-run the anti-AI-writing pass if any narration sentence changes.
